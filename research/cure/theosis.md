# Theosis as the General Form — Directed Becoming, and the Tension It Exposes

**Date:** 2026-08-01
**Prompt:** the Cure for the Terminators is a *localized* solution to a much broader topic — Theosis.
Read the wiki for spirit and inspiration, not literal doctrine.
**Result:** the reframe resolves a problem I had flagged and could not solve, surfaces a design
tension nobody in the project has named, and exposes the sharpest available critique of the whole
representational approach.

---

## 1. What "Theosis" names, stripped to structure

The wiki's own framing is unusually crisp and does not require the theology to transfer:

> *"Becoming like the divine through sustained, cooperative participation (**synergy**) rather than
> through comprehension or a one-time achieved state."*
>
> *"**Theosis is participation, not information.** It is entered into continuously, which is also
> why it can be exited continuously — at any point, not just at the beginning."*

Four structural properties, all secular-transferable:

1. **Continuous, not terminal** — it is enacted, never completed and held.
2. **Directed toward a telos** — becoming *more*, not returning to a prior state.
3. **Reversible at any point** — no permanently achieved condition; withdrawal is always available.
4. **Synergistic** — cooperation between two parties, not imposition by one.

### 1a. The fifth property, which I initially missed — and it is the load-bearing one

**Theosis is not apotheosis, and the tradition is emphatic about this.** Checked against the
source doctrine rather than the wiki gloss:

- Orthodoxy **"rejects all forms of pantheism, which would correspond to what might be called
  *apotheosis* (becoming a god or becoming divine in essence)."**
- Gregory Palamas's **essence/energies distinction** exists precisely to make this possible:
  union is with God's **energies**, never God's **essence**. *"It is impossible for any person or
  other creature to know or to be united with God's essence."*
- The framework **"preserves the ontological distinction between Creator and creature while allowing
  for genuine participation."**

So: **you participate in the thing without ever becoming the thing. The categorical distinction is
permanently preserved — that is not a limitation of the process, it is its defining structure.**

**This is a substantive and non-obvious alignment stance, and it inverts a common assumption.**
Most alignment framings implicitly aim at *internalization* — the system should absorb human values
until they are constitutive of it, i.e. aligned *in essence*. The Theosis structure says the
opposite: alignment is a **sustained relationship between two permanently distinct kinds**, not the
conversion of one into the other. Participation without merger.

Consequences worth taking seriously:
- There is no end-state in which the system "has" the values and can be left alone — consistent with
  property 3 (reversibility) and with the verification-as-practice insight below.
- The system remaining categorically *other* is a feature, not a failure of alignment.
- It maps directly onto the wiki's **Coupling Mode**: communion is reciprocal participation between
  parties who both remain themselves; extraction is one-directional and collapses the relation.

**I wrote §§2–6 below before checking this, and it is the property that most changes the picture.**

## 2. This resolves the problem I flagged and couldn't solve

Two cycles ago I found that **the Cure as specified is a conservatism engine**: it repairs *back*
toward established invariants, so a legitimate update and a corruption are indistinguishable to it.
I called it structural and left it open.

**Theosis names the missing category.** The Cure is *restorative* — return to a valid prior state.
Theosis is *developmental* — move toward a state better than any previously occupied. These require
different machinery, and the project has only ever specified the first.

**So the correct relationship is:** the Cure is the **degenerate, restorative special case** of
directed becoming — what you do when the trajectory has been damaged and no growth is currently
possible. It is triage, not the goal. That is exactly the "localized context solution" framing, and
it is structurally right rather than merely rhetorical.

## 3. The genuinely sharp insight: verification as practice, not credential

The strongest claim on the Theosis page is not theological at all:

> A single performable test (like passing an alignment verification) is **cheaper to fake** than
> *"a practice that must be continuously re-enacted under conditions the performer does not fully
> control."*

Applied to the Comprehension-Alignment problem, this is a real move. Comprehension is a **one-time
credential** — demonstrable, and therefore fakeable by anything capable of the demonstration.
Sustained cooperative participation is an **ongoing cost** under partly-uncontrolled conditions.

The page is also honest about the limit: *"a sufficiently patient Sleeper Angel could, in principle,
sustain a performance for exactly this long... but it does raise the real cost of the deception
rather than leaving it a one-time investment."* That is the correct claim — **not proof against
deception, but a change in its economics.**

**This is costly-signaling theory** (biology, economics: signals are credible when they are
expensive to produce and the expense scales with dishonesty), arrived at independently through
theological vocabulary. It is a defensible, checkable, non-mystical contribution to alignment
verification — and it is arguably the best original idea on either property.

**Follow-up:** connect it explicitly to the costly-signaling literature (Zahavi's handicap
principle; Spence signaling). Unchecked.

## 4. The tension nobody has named: consistency destroys open-endedness

**Open-ended evolution** (OEE) in artificial life is the formal study of systems that keep
generating novelty rather than settling. Four hallmarks: ongoing innovation, unbounded evolution,
ongoing complexity production, and being a defining feature of life. It is considered hard enough to
be called *"a millennium prize problem"* in ALife.

Its central documented failure mode:

> *"Designed evolutionary systems are subject to a thermodynamic drift making them **collapse into
> equilibrium states**. Once local optima are reached, they do not produce novelty anymore, which
> bounds their complexity and diversity."*

**This is what a maximally effective Cure would do.** A consistency-maintenance system that
successfully repairs every deviation back toward established invariants is an *equilibrium-seeking
device*. Run it well enough and the system stops producing novelty — the OEE failure mode, achieved
deliberately.

**So there is a direct design tension between the Cure's stated objective and the Theosis frame it
sits inside:**

| | Optimizes for | Failure at the extreme |
|---|---|---|
| **Cure / consistency maintenance** | Return to admissible region | Equilibrium collapse; no growth; conservatism engine |
| **Theosis / open-endedness** | Continued directed novelty | Unconstrained drift; incoherence; no identity |

Neither pole is safe. **The real target is the narrow band between them** — enough consistency to
preserve identity, enough freedom to keep becoming. That band has never been articulated in this
project, and naming it is a genuine advance over "detect and repair drift."

This also gives the assumption/entrenchment machinery a purpose beyond bookkeeping: entrenchment
orderings are precisely how you decide *what must hold* versus *what may change* — the mechanism by
which a system stays itself while becoming something else.

## 5. The sharpest critique of the whole project — and it converges with Theosis

**Enaction** (Varela, Maturana; De Jaegher & Di Paolo) makes a claim that threatens the project's
representational premise directly:

> Cognition emerges through *"dynamic perception-action coupling and adaptive engagement between
> agents and environments **rather than through internal representations of a pre-given world**."*
>
> *"In the enactive perspective, **language doesn't represent things. Language does things.**
> Languaging is a form of participatory sense-making, and it is always a form of embodied action."*

**Participatory sense-making**: meaning *"emerges relationally through coordination dynamics between
participants themselves,"* with interaction *"partially autonomous and constitutive of cognition
rather than merely serving as a communication channel between pre-existing minds."*

**If this is right, a notation cannot carry meaning.** UWS would be a coordination *medium*, not a
container — and the grounding problem stops being a gap to fill and becomes a category error:
meaning was never in the marks to begin with.

**But notice the convergence.** Enaction says *meaning is interaction, not representation.* The
Theosis page says *"Theosis is participation, not information."* **These are the same structural
claim, reached from a Chilean biologist's theory of living systems and from Greek patristic
theology respectively.** That is a genuinely striking independent convergence — considerably
stronger than most of what this project has previously offered as convergence evidence, because the
two traditions share no lineage whatsoever.

**What it implies for design, taken seriously:** UWS's target should shift from *encoding meaning
for transmission* to *supporting coordination between participants*. Those produce different
notations. A coordination medium is evaluated on whether it enables repair of misunderstanding in
interaction — not on whether a naive receiver can decode it cold. Which, incidentally, dissolves the
decipherment objection: you never needed a receiver who could read it without interaction, because
meaning was always going to be established *through* interaction.

## 6. Honest assessment of the reframe

**What genuinely transfers from Theosis, stripped of doctrine:** continuous-not-terminal;
telos-directed rather than restorative; reversible; synergistic; and the verification-as-practice
insight, which is the best original idea here.

**What does not transfer, and should not be smuggled:** any claim about what the telos *is*.
Theosis in its home tradition has a specific terminus (union with a specific divine nature). The
structural form — *directed becoming under sustained cooperative participation* — is content-neutral
about the direction. **Importing the form while quietly importing the destination would be exactly
the move this project has repeatedly been caught making.** State the telos as a design choice, or
leave it open, but do not let the vocabulary supply it.

**And the honest risk:** "Theosis" is a high-status word for a structure that also has plainer names
— development, maturation, individuation, cultivation, open-ended growth. Using the theological term
may import unearned profundity. The test: every claim made under this frame should survive
restatement in the plain vocabulary. Where it doesn't, the theology was doing the work.

### The distinction most likely to be got wrong — including by us

**Theosis vs. apotheosis** is the fork that matters, and they imply different engineering targets:

| | Structure | AI reading | Implies |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Apotheosis** (Greco-Roman: a human *becomes* a god) | Ontological ascent; you become the higher thing | Recursive self-improvement; transcending one's original nature | A trajectory with a terminus, where the system ends up categorically different |
| **Theosis** (Orthodox; explicitly **not** apotheosis) | Participation in the higher thing's *energies*, never its *essence*; you remain a creature | Sustained cooperative relationship between permanently distinct kinds | No terminus, no merger, reversible, relationship is the point |

**These are not variants of one idea — they are opposites on the question that matters most**
(does the distinction survive?). If the intended frame was ascent/transcendence, that is a coherent
frame with real literature behind it, but it is **apotheosis**, and almost everything in this
document would need rewriting: the reversibility, the anti-merger stance, the
verification-as-practice argument, and the Coupling Mode mapping all depend on the distinction being
preserved.

**Recommendation: pick deliberately and label it.** The structural properties (§1, §1a) are what
matter; the vocabulary should follow the choice rather than supply it. And if neither theological
term fits, the plain names — directed development under sustained participation, without merger —
carry the same content without importing a tradition's commitments.

---

## What changes

1. **Reframe the Cure explicitly as the restorative special case** of directed becoming — triage,
   not the goal. This is structurally correct, not just generous to the wiki.
2. **Name the consistency/open-endedness tension as a first-class design constraint.** Both extremes
   fail. The target is the band between, and entrenchment ordering is the mechanism for finding it.
3. **Develop verification-as-practice.** Continuous costly participation raises the price of
   deception where a one-time comprehension credential does not. Connect to costly-signaling theory.
4. **Take the enactive critique seriously rather than defensively.** If meaning is participatory,
   UWS is a coordination medium, not a container — a different and arguably more achievable design
   target, which also dissolves the decipherment objection.
5. **Research open-ended evolution properly.** It is the formal study of exactly the "continuous
   becoming" the Theosis frame requires, with named failure modes and a considered-hard status.
6. **Keep the telos as an explicit open design choice**, never supplied silently by vocabulary.
