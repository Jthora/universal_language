## 1. Before searching  ← written first (S2)

**The hypothesis under test — `NOTATIONS-SUCCEED-DOMAIN-FIRST`:**

> **Notations built for a domain became universal. Notations that announced universality failed —
> unless institutional compulsion carried them.**

**Predictions:** (1) adoption was practitioner-driven, not decreed; (2) SI/metric is the known

**The adversarial query, named in advance (R1):** *are these notations actually cross-cultural, or

distribution. That error is already twice in `FAILURES.md`.

**Falsifier, stated before sorting anything (T12):** a notation that announced universality, had no
institutional compulsion, and became universal anyway — **or a domain-first notation that succeeded
because institutions carried it.**

**Stop condition:** four queries.

## 3. The falsification, stated plainly

**Labanotation is a domain-first notation whose adoption was institutional, not organic:**

> *"The adoption of Labanotation was significantly advanced through **institutional support**."* The
> **Dance Notation Bureau** (New York, 1940), the **International Council of Kinetography Laban**
> (1959), and a journal running 1957–1994.

**That is the falsifier I wrote in section 1, met exactly.** Domain-first did not mean bottom-up.

**And the "survivors" are not universal.** The adversarial queries hit both headliners:

> **Music** — *"The universality or hegemony of Western music notation is **not a given**, but is
> contingent on processes of **transformation, mobilization, dissemination and stabilization**"* —
> *"**socially constructed** rather than objectively superior."* It *"struggles to represent
> **microtones and complex rhythms**… Arabic maqam and Balkan odd meters."*
>
> **Mathematics** — ethnomathematics disputes it outright; and note where even the *pro*-universality
> camp concedes. The **Culturally Independence Thesis** holds that mathematics is universal
> *"**independent from its symbolizations**"* — i.e. **the content may be universal and the notation
> is granted to be contingent.**

**Both headline survivors are contested, and contested in the same place: the notation encodes its
culture's choices.** I predicted this query would be uncomfortable and it was more so than expected.

### 3a. The steelman (R6) — and it changes the verdict from "dead" to "not general"

I wrote "dead" first. **R6 says state the steelman before the kill, and running it does not support
that word.**

**Mathematical notation is the hypothesis's surviving case, and it is a strong one.** There was no
Dance Notation Bureau for `∫`. Euler *"released constantly, shared everything, and corresponded
prodigiously"*; the Analytical Society was a **student society at Cambridge**, not a body that owned
a standard. **Adoption really was practitioner-driven with no institutional vehicle.**

So the correct statement is narrower and more interesting:

| Notation | Institutional vehicle | Outcome |
|---|---|---|
| **Mathematical** | **none** — correspondence and printers | broad adoption |
| **Labanotation** | **Bureau, Council, journal** | adoption within its field, never beyond |

> **The universally-quantified hypothesis is falsified; the mechanism is real but conditional.** What
> plausibly discriminates: whether **non-adopters are punished by illegibility.** England could not
> read European mathematics. **A choreographer who does not read Labanotation loses nothing** — the
> dance is transmitted body to body regardless.
>
> **`NOTATIONS-SUCCEED-DOMAIN-FIRST` is not dead. It is unquantified**, and the missing variable is
> the cost of not adopting.

**Revival condition (R4):** the strong form returns if a domain-first notation is found that achieved
broad cross-field adoption with **no institutional vehicle and no illegibility penalty** — which
would show the mechanism is not the discriminator either.

**Recorded rather than quietly dropped, because a preregistered hypothesis that fails is data — and
because the first word I reached for was wrong.**

## 4. What replaced it, and it is worth more

### 4a. The universality question was mis-posed — and this is the user's point, confirmed sharply

**No notation is universal.** So *"did it achieve universality?"* sorts every case into the same
bin and destroys the information. The question that preserves it:

> **What is this notation's domain of validity, and what capped it?**

**Every cap in the record is specific, different, and diagnosable:**

| Notation | Cap | Kind of error |
|---|---|---|
| **Staff notation** | 12-tone equal temperament, metrical division | **hard-coded a discretization of a continuum** |
| **Solresol** | turned relations into vocabulary (`037`) | destroyed the part that travelled |
| **Wilkins' Real Character** | required a complete prior taxonomy of the world | unbounded prerequisite |
| **Blissymbolics** | — | **succeeded in its domain; only "failed" against a bar it was measured by afterwards** |

**Four caps, four mechanisms, and not one of them is "universality is impossible."** `037` aggregated
these into *"four serious attempts across three centuries. None achieved universality"* — **a uniform
negative manufactured from heterogeneous, fixable, individually informative failures.**

### 4b. The recurring cap is the error we already caught ourselves making

**Staff notation fails on microtones because microtones are the notes between the lines.** It
hard-coded a discretization of a continuous quantity — pitch — and inherited that grid's blind spots
permanently.

`033` found exactly this in our own work: `{Z, C, P, V}` was *"a discretization I imposed on a
function space"*, and *"there is no canonical finite partition of a function space."*

> **Staff notation is the historical instance of the mistake this project already caught itself
> making — and it is the mistake that capped the most successful constructed notation in history.**

**And it is an argument for the fixed point.** A rotation system carries **no metric discretization
at all** — it is purely topological, with nothing playing the role of equal temperament. **The
property that capped staff notation is the one property our fixed point structurally lacks.**

That is not an argument that UWS will work. It is a named failure mode, historically demonstrated at
scale, that the current design does not have.

### 4c. Adoption is a network effect — our inference, not a finding (T11)

Mathematical notation's mechanism is clean and it is neither "decree" nor "merit":

> *"The standardization wasn't a matter of decree but rather emerged through practical adoption."*
> The **Analytical Society** (Cambridge, 1830s) formed *"specifically to promote the Leibniz
> notation… because the Leibniz notation was being used throughout Europe, and **students in England
> were not able to easily understand the newest work being done by European mathematicians**."*

**England switched because Newton's notation had made them illegible to their own field.** Not
authority, not virtue — **the cost of being unreadable.**

**Marked as inference, not finding:** this reads a mechanism across ethnomusicology, history of
mathematics and dance-notation history — **different fields, different methods, no study compared
them.** T11 applies and it is my inference.
