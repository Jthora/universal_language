## 1. What was held

That the necessity question was settled negatively. Zadrozny (1994) proves bare compositionality is
formally vacuous — for any meaning assignment a re-encoding exists making it compositional —
therefore no primitive inventory is forced, therefore **UL can only be a bootstrapping protocol**:
something two parties agree on, not something that exists.

Peirce's triadic sign was also treated as foreclosing the question, on the grounds that an
interpretant is required for signification.

## 2. What broke it

Direct pushback: *"Universal Language may be emergent. The primitives are clues. Emergence is the
key. You basically just gave up."*

Checking that produced the keystone, from statistical physics:

> *"The critical exponents are not visible in the microscopic description. A complete Hamiltonian for
> water does not contain them."*

Water and uniaxial magnets — different atoms, different force laws — share **identical** critical
exponents. Class membership is set by dimensionality and order-parameter symmetry; everything else
is formally an *irrelevant operator*. And symmetry at a fixed point can **exceed** the symmetry of
the microscopic model.

**So universal structure can exist that is not derivable from its constituents.**

## 3. What replaced it

Three kinds of universality, where two had been assumed:

| | Status |
|---|---|
| Logical necessity | Dead — Zadrozny |
| **Emergent universality** | **The claim.** Never previously considered |
| Convention | Too weak — the retreat position |

Promoted to `framework/emergent-universality.md`. Claim `UL-IS-EMERGENT-UNIVERSAL` added at
priority 0.

## 4. Why it survived as long as it did

**Zadrozny's scope was dropped.** It is a result about the *microscopic* level and says nothing
about what emerges under coarse-graining. Applying it globally was an error of level, not of rigour.

And Peirce's interpretant requirement is a **definitional stipulation**, not a theorem — it was
allowed to foreclose an ontological question it does not address.

**Generalized:** a researcher's conclusion was treated as the edge of the map rather than a data
point on it. That pattern recurred five more times before being caught structurally in `008`.
