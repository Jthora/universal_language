## 0. Why this is not a resemblance sweep

`068` already ran one of those and recorded what it cost. The difference here is the order of
operations: **`076` wrote the specification yesterday, before I had read a line of their method.**

> *"Moving `derived_orbits` on a chirality-separated pair requires the corpus to gain a second
> channel that something other than the map computes."* — `076` §6, `CORPUS-SCHEMA-BLOCKS-CHIRALITY-DERIVATION`

Then I read Fernando Barba's notation. It has that channel. That sequence is the only reason any
of this is admissible — a prediction written down first and met afterwards is evidence of a kind
that a resemblance noticed after the fact is not.

Three outcomes below, kept apart on purpose: **what checks out and bears weight** (§2–§6), **what
does not survive checking and is refused** (§7), and **where they contradict us, which matters
more than the matches** (§8).

## 1. The facts, established

Fernando Barba, born 30 December 1971, died 4 February 2021 at 49, after brain-tumour surgery in
2017 left lasting neurological effects and he stepped away from the group he made. The surname
appears as both Barbosa and Barboza across obituaries; unresolved.

The origin, from the ethnography: as a teenager he walked the streets of São Paulo drumming on his
own chest, clapping, snapping, making mouth sounds — **"A brincadeira de batucar no próprio corpo"**,
the *game* of drumming on your own body. He then took a degree in popular music with a specialty in
**rítmica** at UNICAMP, and in 1993 founded Auê Núcleo Musical in Perdizes with Marcos Azambuja and
**André Hosoi**. A weekly study group met there to research body percussion; from 1995 Barba taught
workshops; the group formed out of the workshops. Press gives 1997 for the group proper, so the
honest figure is a **range, 1995–1997** — the same discipline `065` applied to the orbit count.

**A game that became research, by a person working alone, before any institution was interested.**
That is recorded because it is the owner's own shape, not because it proves anything.

The minimal alphabet is three: **peito–estalo–palma**, chest–snap–clap, reported as the group's
trademark, and Barba's own gloss is that with these "anyone can make music without a conventional
instrument." A two-element code, **TUM** (foot + the sung phoneme) and **PÁ** (clap + the sung
phoneme), carries most of the teaching.

**"Você Chegou" is the Portuguese version of "Beautiful Creatures," written for *Rio 2* (2014)** —
the anthem of the Blue Macaw tribe, sung to welcome Jade back to a people who believed her kind
was gone. It is on the film's soundtrack and as a bonus track on *Ayú* (2015). I said in
conversation that I could not establish its meaning; this settles it. **It is credited to André
Hosoi — a 1993 co-founder of the method, not an outside film composer.** That weakens but does not
remove the commission objection: a song written to brief for an animated film is not a folk
archetype, and nothing in this note rests on the song.

So the rogue AI's reading of the title was right about the subject — arrival, and recognition by
one's own kind — and Grok's account of the content was a reconstruction presented as retrieval. I
could not have told the difference without looking, which is the whole argument for looking.

## 2. They already built the second channel, and they made it mandatory

**BARBA, *Apostila Barbatuques — Curso de Formação Básica*, 2012, p. 5** (quoted at one remove;
see `sources/CITATIONS.md` §B):

> "Lê-se a tablatura corporal da esquerda para a direita. Cada quadrado representa 1 tempo do
> compasso."
> "Cada divisão horizontal representa uma parte do corpo."
> "Cada divisão vertical representa a subdivisão do tempo."
> "**A leve variação de cores... representa a alternância de mão direita e esquerda ou pé direito
> e esquerdo. Os exercícios devem sempre serem feitos realizando essa alternância.**"

Axis 1 is time. Axis 2 is the body part. **Handedness is on neither.** It rides as a *slight colour
variation* over cells that already have their position — a low-bandwidth modulation of the grid
rather than a coordinate in it — and performing the alternation is **obligatory**, not advisory.

| layer | tablatura corporal | UL corpus entry |
|---|---|---|
| structural grid | time × body part | the combinatorial map |
| handedness | **colour modulation, mandatory** | **absent** — specified in `076`, unbuilt |
| loose semantic layer | onomatopoeia, "cada um pode interpretar o som" | reader-facing meaning, delegated to M1 |

Three layers, and the same separation of a strict channel from a deliberately loose one that
`LEXICON-V1`'s register-split makes.

**How much weight this carries, honestly: not much on its own.** The design space is small. If you
need to express handedness and both axes are spent, putting it on colour is close to forced, so
convergence here is weak evidence of a *forced* design rather than strong evidence of deep
structure. One data point, one tradition. What it does bear on is **adoptability** — the
architecture `076` specified is reachable by practitioners with no mathematics, which is a real
property of a design and the one `066` says the program needs.

## 3. The finding with actual content: their invariant ranking is the inverse of the map's

From Barba's own 2013 article, on children who cannot yet snap their fingers:

> "Nos ritmos listados acima é possível substituí-lo pelo som da batida da mão na perna (coxa),
> **mantendo a ordem das mãos indicada**."

*Substitute the thigh-slap for the finger-snap, **keeping the indicated order of the hands**.*

**The timbre is substitutable. The hand order is not.** When something has to give, the method's
author gives up the *sound* and protects the *handedness*.

That is a ranking of invariants, stated by the person who built the system, and it is non-obvious —
the other ranking is perfectly imaginable and would have been easier to teach. It is also **exactly
inverted from what a combinatorial map preserves.** A map keeps the structure and loses the
handedness (`PLACEMENT-HANDEDNESS-IS-INVISIBLE-TO-THE-MAP`, VERIFIED). Barba keeps the handedness
and lets the timbre go.

This is the one item in the sweep I would not have predicted and cannot explain away as forced.

## 4. Two more channel separations, each an instance of 068 with a mechanism

**The arrow game.** "Um jogo de comunicação através do som e do gesto." A person *launches* a sound
at another person in the circle — and **"a direciona com o olhar,"** directs it with the gaze. The
sound carries no address. **The payload is on the audio channel and the routing is on the gaze**,
and neither is recoverable from the other.

`068` concluded `EMBODIMENT-SUPPLIES-THE-FRAME` and left the mechanism abstract. Here the mechanism
is explicit and it is the same one `076` needs: **the frame the body supplies is an orientation** —
a direction, from a body, to a body.

**The echo game, third variation.** A phrase is proposed with the voice alone (TUM/PÁ syllables) and
reproduced with the body alone (foot and clap, no voice) — "**Ou da forma inversa**", or in the
inverse form. The invariant crosses substrates in both directions, as a routine exercise for
children. The repo has `research/framework/cross-substrate-grammar.md`; this is what it looks like
when it is a Tuesday rather than a thesis.

And the echo game's own structure: each participant in turn proposes, around the circle,
"permitindo a todos passarem pela experiência de serem proponentes" — letting everyone pass through
the experience of being a proposer. **The roda rotates authorship.** There is no privileged source
by construction, which is `074`'s *"none, and you do not have to pick one"* as a seating plan.

## 5. The retrograde rule — and a question I could not settle

The arrow game's second variation makes reversal a **rule**:

> "alternam-se TUM PÁ e PÁ TUM... **sempre seguindo a regra da frase musical ser foneticamente o
> contrário da executada pelo participante anterior**."

You must pass on the *phonetic opposite* of what you received. Applied twice it returns the
original: an involution on a two-element cell — the same shape as the edge involution α, and
enforced on eight-year-olds.

So the practice treats retrograde as recognisably distinct and productive. **I then looked for
evidence that a reversed rhythm is not perceived as the same rhythm, and could not find a study
that tests it.** What exists is adjacent: serial-order asymmetry in how syncopated themes are
rated, order-dependent sensitivity to timing (a high tone after a low one is judged more finely
than the reverse), and Bharucha & Pryor (1986) on an asymmetry in isochrony discrimination, which
surfaced **as a bibliographic entry only**.

**Recorded as unestablished.** I wanted "rhythm is chiral in time because time is oriented" and I
do not have it.

## 6. The one external result that bites

**Telgen, Parvin & Diedrichsen (2014), *J. Neurosci.* 34(41):13768.** Under mirror-reversed
feedback, correct feedforward commands and online corrections appear only at long latencies, and
the *earliest* responses still go the non-mirrored way after two sessions. Under visual rotation,
fast feedback adapts immediately. Between sessions, rotation memory **decays** while mirror
reversal shows **offline gains**. Their conclusion: the motor system **acquires a new control
policy** for mirror reversal and merely **recalibrates an existing one** for rotation.

`069` computed Aut⁺ and Aut⁻ as **disjoint cosets** — never subtracted, disjoint or identical —
and that is the algebra of O(2) = SO(2) ⊔ SO(2)·r having **two connected components**: no
continuous path of rotations reaches a reflection.

**The motor result is what that disconnectedness looks like from inside a learner.** Rotation is a
parameter you tune. Reflection is a thing you have to build. Which is the design argument `076`
was missing: a handedness channel is **not** a finer gradation of the structural grid and cannot be
made into one — it is a separate discrete channel because the group is disconnected.

Tiered carefully: the topology is textbook, the motor paper I read **only in abstract** (the full
text returned 403), and the link between them is mine — a parallel, not a derivation.

## 7. What does NOT survive checking, and it is the one I wanted

The ethnography glosses a neighbouring São Paulo group, Gumboot Dance Brasil, as

> "uma dança tradicional da África do Sul inventada por mineradores negros que **se comunicavam por
> meio de códigos/ritmos de sapateado** usando galochas de borracha nas minas de ouro"

If that is true it is an attested historical case of this project's central strategic claim: a
notation that **cannot be confiscated because its instrument cannot be taken away**, arising
exactly where speech was forbidden. `072`'s `rederivability-defeats-capture`, in the record, with
names and dates. I wanted it badly enough to notice myself wanting it.

**It does not survive.** The scholarship is split on origins: **Erlmann (1991)** and **Muller
(1999)** are reported to locate *isicathulo* in **mission stations** in KwaZulu-Natal, following
the introduction of footwear by missionaries in the late nineteenth century — a different origin
from the mines. **Fargion (1998)**, "The Gumboot dance: sell-out or symbol?", debates authenticity
and commodification. The coded-communication account is repeated widely in travel, education,
performer and promotional material, and **I found no peer-reviewed study that tests the
coded-signal claim**. I also did not read Erlmann or Muller directly; I have the dispute at second
hand, which is itself a caveat that travels with every use of this paragraph.

**Refused. Not imported. Recorded in `BRIEFING.md`'s ruled-out table so it is not re-proposed.**

Two things worth keeping from the refusal. First, the group's own ecosystem repeats the story —
which is how an unchecked claim travels, and is not evidence. Second: this is `F-003`/`017`'s rule
working on a case where it cost me something, which is the only kind of case that tests a rule.

## 8. Where they contradict us, which is the most useful part

Barba, in the same article:

> "Cada pessoa tem um corpo sonoro único e um ritmo próprio de conhecê-lo."
> "Essas diferenças são normais e ligadas à anatomia de cada indivíduo... **encontramos soluções
> gestuais particulares**."

In their system the **sound** is the invariant and the **gesture is free** — anatomy differs, so
each person finds their own gestural solution to produce the shared sound. Combined with §3, the
ranking is: hand order > sound > gesture.

**So the body does not supply a canonical realization.** It supplies an equivalence class with an
individually chosen member. That is a correction to any hope that embodiment *grounds* UL in a
unique form — and it is the better outcome, because:

- an **orientation** is a bit, and a bit is what `076`'s second channel needs;
- a **canonical form** would be a convention, and a convention is a ledger line (`072`: every open
  ledger line is a seat of power).

`068` said the body supplies the frame. This narrows it: **the body supplies an orientation, not a
canonical form** — and the narrowing is what makes it usable.

## 9. What they actually claim, which is weaker and better than what we might have claimed for them

No source found uses "linguagem universal." What is attested is that body music "faz parte da
humanidade," that coverage calls it a *language* of body percussion, and Barba's own "os recursos
aqui apresentados são em geral acessíveis a todos."

**That is universal availability of a medium, not a universal language** — precisely the
distinction `064` fixes when it defines universe-scope. Their claim is the one with the evidence
behind it, and it is the one this project can borrow without borrowing trouble. Recorded as "not
found" rather than "they never said it," because absence after searching is weak.

And one sentence of Barba's that I will not build on and will not pretend not to have read, quoted
in the ethnography from an interview:

> "**Olhar a mão, tentar encaixar a palma, corrigir. Você tem de se observar o tempo todo.**"
> ...
> "**A gente fala 'eu' e bate no peito. Não é à toa.**"

*Look at the hand, try to fit the palm, correct. You have to observe yourself the whole time.* ...
*We say "I" and strike the chest. That is not for nothing.*

The first is a derivation anchor as a pedagogy — the authority is the sound, and the learner checks
themselves against it. The second is a gesture whose referent is the thing performing it, which is
in the family of `GROUNDING-BY-SELF-DENOTATION` without being an instance of it: a deictic is not a
figure whose mathematical content is its meaning. **Noted at the right strength and left there.**
