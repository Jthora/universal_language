## 4. Findings

### 4a. The two-tier structure is in the data — with the boundary in a different place (P1)

**Natural tier, human-universal:** Mundurukú children and adults — no formal schooling, no
geometric vocabulary — *"spontaneously made use of basic geometric concepts such as points, lines,
parallelism, or right angles"* (Dehaene, Izard, Pica & Spelke 2006). **Core geometry exists without
education. "Concepts that require no core explanation" is a documented finding, not a hope.**

**But the boundary moved, exactly as preregistered:** the geometric-regularity effect holds in
**all humans** *"regardless of age, culture and education"* — **and is absent in baboons**
(Sablé-Meyer et al., PNAS 2021). The field's own explanation for the dissociation is *"a language
of thought for the mental representation of geometric shapes"* — **discrete symbolic recombination.
The formal tier is the explanation of the human side of the boundary.**

> **So A1 is confirmed as structure and corrected as scope: the two tiers exist, and the boundary
> between them is an empirical variable the field is actively mapping — not a given.** "Most things
> and lifeforms" holds for motion and intuitive physics (not directly searched — flagged); for
> geometric regularity the tested boundary currently sits *inside* primates. The auditory piece
> lands where `036` already put it: **the relational part of music travels cross-culturally; the
> conventional part does not** — the ledger's boundary, found independently years apart.

### 4b. The alignment mechanism exists and is format acquisition, not purification (P2 + P3)

**P2:** Guugu Yimithirr speakers — cardinal directions only, no left/right — *"predominantly code
for **nonverbal memory** in 'absolute' concepts congruent with their language."* Levinson:
*"Whorfian effects may in fact be demonstrable in the spatial domain."* **The strongest documented
case of language restructuring cognition is spatial — UL's home domain.** (Contested — Li &
Gleitman's task-effect critique is known and did not surface here; S8 debt recorded.)

**P3, the one designed to hurt, confirmed:** *"far transfer remains a chimera"* (Sala & Gobet
2017) — effect sizes **inversely related to design quality**, consistent with Thorndike &
Woodworth's common-elements theory since 1901.

> **The strong reading of A3 — learn UL, become generally purified — is dead on current evidence,
> and we record that without flinching (REQ-3).** What survives is stronger than it sounds:
> **format-scoped alignment.** The Levinson effect is not far transfer — it is the acquired format
> *becoming the representation* within its domain. UL's domain is space, structure, relation,
> computation.

**And the graveyard's own headstone theory cuts our way — recorded as OUR inference (T11):**
common-elements theory says transfer happens exactly where elements are shared. **A language whose
elements are the universe's invariants shares elements with every domain by construction.** By the
very theory that killed formal discipline, a language of universal elements is the unique best case
for broad transfer. No study has tested it; it is the *prediction* the thesis makes.

### 4d. "Quantum" has an honest baseline meaning, and the substrate already has it (P5)

types**… the idea of utilizing linear typing in this context **pioneered by Selinger and

discipline: multiplication required an explicit `Erase` agent because *"this discarding of the
second argument **must be made explicit**."* Copying, likewise, is an explicit duplicator agent —
never free.

> **"Quantum," at baseline: the operational layer's resource discipline is the universe's own — no
> free copy (no-cloning), no free delete (no-deleting), enforced structurally.** Interaction nets

> quantum-compatible by construction.** Literal quantum execution remains a registered conjecture —
