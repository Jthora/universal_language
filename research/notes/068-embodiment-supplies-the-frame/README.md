## 1. The hazard, named before anything is taken from it

The analysis supplied reads the mark (a dotted circle above three radiating lines) as a human
figure, then places it in a family: Vitruvian Man, the circumpunct, solar symbols, shamanic
figures, chakras, Aboriginal dot motifs, alchemical symbols. It says the mark *"accidentally
resonates with many ancient symbolic systems."*

**That is this project's documented failure mode, exactly** — `F-003` and note `017` record
retrofitting correspondences as the characteristic error here, and note `063` was written against
it. The analysis also undercuts its own framing: the stated origin is a **2022 Brazilian studio
brand identity**, developed from dance and movement experiments. So the ancient family is a
post-hoc reading laid over a modern logo, and *"accidentally resonates"* is carrying weight that
evidence should carry.

**Nothing from the resemblance sweep is imported.** What follows uses only the decomposition and
the repo's own derived results.

## 2. The decomposition, and where it stops

In the register's actual vocabulary the mark has two parts:

- **`●` nested in `○`.** "Point inside Circle" is *literally the attestation* for the `nest`
  operator in the imported register, and one of three attestations for `superpose_concentric`.
  (That the same figure attests two operators is the register's own ambiguity, not an artifact of
  this reading.)
- **`\ | /` — three Lines meeting at a junction, each with a free end.** That is
  `JUNCTION-WITH-FREE-ENDS`, one of the five machine-verified entries in `LEXICON-V1`.

So a commercial design studio with no knowledge of this project produced a figure whose parts sit
entirely inside the attested set. That is mildly interesting and is *not* the finding.

**The finding is where the decomposition stops.** The mark reads as *a human* only because the
circle is **above** the lines. Up/down. And up/down is one of exactly three relational schemas the
operator set **provably cannot express frame-free**: `directional-schemas-need-a-declared-frame`
(derived) measures UP/DOWN drifting **2.20** and FRONT/BACK **4.40** under global canvas rotation,
against **machine zero** (≤1.3e-15) for every attested operator
(`attested-operators-are-frame-free`, derived).

> **The decomposable part is frame-free and inside the set. The part that makes it a *person* is
> frame-bound and outside it. The human figure is not in the geometry — it is in the frame the
> viewer brings.**

This is the instrument doing the work note `049` said it would: *every cap is specific and
diagnosable.* Here it diagnoses precisely where the mystical reading originates — not in the
symbol, but in having a body with a head on top. And `direction-is-read-as-contrast` (derived)
says the rest: **no meaning in this notation rests on which way is up.**

## 3. So human inclusion has a derived role, and it was already written down

`064` demoted human universals from warrant to corroboration, which left "human inclusion" with no
principled job — present as motivation, absent as structure. It has one:

> **The body supplies the frame that frame-bound schemas require.**

A plane has no privileged up. A *canvas* is a plane plus a frame, and an embodied reader is one
source of that frame — gravity pins it physically, a head-and-feet axis pins it perceptually. So
the three schemas UL cannot say frame-free (UP/DOWN, FRONT/BACK, PATH) become sayable to an
embodied reader *without further convention*, because the reader is the frame.

**The repo already had this sentence, sitting unnoticed in a `scope:` field** — the Aether/Nether
gloss *"holds only GIVEN that up and down are meaningful on the page — **an embedding assumption
inherited from the reader's body**, not a property of the plane."* It was recorded as a *caveat*.
Under universe-scope it is better read as a *mechanism*: the natural tier's contribution is
embodiment, and embodiment's specific contribution is a frame.

**This is not a retreat from universe-scope, and the distinction matters.** The classification stays
frame-free and therefore universal. Humans are not where its universality comes from. They are a
*frame source* — one of presumably many, since gravity and anisotropic embodiment are not peculiar
to Earth — which is what lets the frame-bound layer be used at all. Registered as
`EMBODIMENT-SUPPLIES-THE-FRAME`.

Note `036` found the same shape empirically: the **relational** part of music travels
cross-culturally, the conventional part does not. Body percussion is that finding's strongest case —
the relational part with the instrument removed entirely.

## 4. The usability correction, which is mine

Barbatuques' thesis is *"the instrument was inside you all along."* The owner's answer to *why
geometry* was *"any caveman can take a stick and draw symbols in the dirt; easy."*

**Those are one claim: zero-equipment expressivity.** And every "do" this session proposed requires
a machine — run the checkers, close the symbol↔dart round trip, canonicalize a structure. The
reasoning was that of a tool-using agent modelling a tool-using reader, and **UL's founding property
is that it needs neither.**

For the five verified meanings the verification is **visual**. Corpus `005` and `006` are the
machine-checked minimal pair: a segment and a triangle, with

```
vertices 5 · edges 4 · components 2 · planar_faces 2 · euler_planar 3 · degree_sequence [1,1,2,2,2]
```

**identical across both.** The entire meaning difference — CONTAINMENT against SEPARATION — is which
region the segment sits in, carried by the nesting relation alone.

A reader tells those apart **instantly, by eye, with no convention and no teacher**, and the machine
agrees by a theorem (Jordan). That is the shape of the first usable act:

> **Not "run this and look at the output." Decide which of two figures means containment — then be
> told, by a theorem rather than by an authority, that you were right.**

The reader's next action differs because they did it: they now hold a meaning they derived rather
than accepted, and the instrument that confirmed it needed no belief in the project. Registered as
`ENTRY-ACT-IS-VISUAL-DISCRIMINATION`.

**And that is `Module N`** — one of the ten curriculum modules the corpus's `teaches` fields cite and
none of which exist. **The usability gap and the curriculum gap are the same gap**, and this is the
first evidence about which direction resolves it: embodied and visual, not computational.

## 5. The external test, and why it is not registered

A corpus of independently designed glyphs — logos, signage, iconography, designed with no knowledge
of this project — decomposed against the attested operator set, is a real experiment and cheap.

**Its ceiling, stated first.** It is a *human-perceptual* convergence test. Caveat `F1-c` applies and
applies harder than it does to Changizi: logos are **selected for human memorability**, so
survivorship bias is in the sampling frame by design. And under `064` human convergence is
corroboration, not warrant. **It cannot touch universality**, and anyone citing it that way is
repeating the error §1 refuses.

**What it can do** is feed `067`'s bounded search. That note re-posed the open question as: *is
frame-freeness plus the symmetry-order bound on bearings sufficient to force the attested set, or is
there a frame-free relation on two figures the corpus does not attest?* If independent designers
reliably reach for a frame-free relation **outside** the set, that is `067`'s falsifier, obtained
cheaply from material that already exists.

**Not registered as a claim, deliberately.** The evidence is n = 1 and the instance arrived through a
resemblance analysis. Registering a prediction on that basis is how §1's pattern gets in through the
side door. It is recorded here as available work with its ceiling attached, and if someone runs it
the result gets a tier.
