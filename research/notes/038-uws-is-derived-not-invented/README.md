# 038 — UWS is a *derived* notation, and I mis-filed it four notes running

**Type:** correction
**Opened:** 2026-08-12
**Status:** closed
**Thread:** corrects `033`, `034`, `035` (partly) and `037`
**Prompted by:** *"UWS is not SUPPOSED to be kind A. It's supposed to be derived. Are there more
kinds, or did you jump the shark?"*

**Both. There are more kinds, and I jumped the shark — four notes ago, not one.**

---

## 1. The taxonomy in `037` collapsed two independent dimensions

I offered three kinds as if they were a list. They are a **grid**:

| | **Invented** | **Derived** | **Discovered** |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Notation** | Leibniz, Wilkins, Blissymbolics, Solresol, Esperanto | **← UWS belongs here** | — |
| **Structure** | — | — | Logos, mathematics as nature's language, universality class |
| **Relation** | — | — | love, participatory sense-making |

**"Notation × derived" is a cell my taxonomy had no room for**, so UWS was forced into "notation ×
invented" purely because it is a notation.

**The classification criterion should have been *how the structure is arrived at*, not *what kind of
object it is*.** UWS is a notation whose structure is *forced* rather than chosen — which is exactly
what distinguishes it from Esperanto and Solresol, both designed by taste.

### And the historical record therefore does not transfer

The 300-year failure record in `037` is a record of **inventions** failing. **Whether a *derived*
notation fares differently is untested, because nobody has derived one.** That cell is empty.

**Neither good news nor bad — but the accurate statement**, and `037` implied the opposite.

## 2. The evidence against my own classification was eleven notes long

`022`, `023`, `024`, `025`, `026`, `028`, `029`, `030`, `031`, `032`, `033` — **every one derives UWS
structure.** Curvature strata, Erlangen survival, the junction axis, the combinatorial map, the table
projections. Those are derivations, not design choices.

**I filed UWS as invented while actively deriving it.** The glossary's phrase *"a constructed
notation"* is where I took it from, but **constructed ≠ arbitrary**, and nothing else supported the
reading.

**One root error, stated four ways: treating UWS as an arbitrary construction rather than a
derivation attempt.**

## 4. And `034`'s evidence was already flagged as inadmissible — by this repo

`034` used **AMR** as the primary evidence that semantic formalisms use labels rather than order.

**The research register already records, at `D2-c`:**

> **AMR is an *"English-only corpus, English predicate lexicon, English annotators."*** Using its
> coverage as universality evidence *"would reproduce UNL's single most documented failure mode."*
> Marked **"NARROWED (near-miss caught)."**

**The error was caught once, recorded, and I walked into it again.** AMR is an annotation convention
for an English corpus, not an attempt at universal structure — so "AMR uses labels" reflects its
design purpose and tells us nothing about what semantic structure must be.

**What survives of `034`:** Conceptual Graphs remain a legitimate data point. **One formalism is not
"independently developed formalisms."** The finding is real but far weaker than recorded, and the
plural was doing unearned work.

## 5. What actually survives

**Undamaged — these do not depend on the framing:**
- **All derivations, `022`–`032`.** Their validity is internal.
- **`ROTATION-MINIMIZES-CONVENTION` (`035`).** The ℤ/2-versus-Sₙ argument stands on its own.
- **The music finding (`036`).** Independent empirical result.
- **The kind-A historical record (`037`).** Those inventions really did fail. What is wrong is
  putting UWS among them.

**Damaged:**
- `034`'s generalization — evidence is one formalism, not a class
- `033`'s demotion of UWS work
- `037`'s classification

**The cut `033` should have made:** not *UWS versus UL*, but **derived structure versus conventional
choices inside UWS.** The glyph-to-concept assignments and the lexicon are conventional. The
curvature classification and the combinatorial map are derived. **Only the first kind is microscopic
detail.**

## 6. Why this happened — the generalizable part

**I built a framework and immediately read a conclusion off it, without checking whether the object
fit the framework.** `037` introduced a taxonomy and classified UWS in the same note, with no test of
fit. `033` introduced a criterion and re-sorted the entire backlog by it in the same note.

**A framework introduced and applied in one motion has never been checked against anything.** The
discipline that exists for *claims* — tier, evidence, falsifier — has no counterpart for *framings*,
and a framing determines what all subsequent claims mean.

**Standing rule (T12): a classification is a claim. State what would show the object does not fit,
before sorting anything by it.** Had `037` asked "what would show UWS is not kind A?", the answer —
*eleven notes deriving its structure* — was already in the repository.
