## Honest synthesis against the falsification condition

**The falsification condition was:** if no case anywhere reaches FULL on the logical cluster or the
structural cluster's embedding/binding criteria, that's a meaningful negative result.

**Result: not falsified, but not comfortably confirmed either — genuinely mixed, and the mixture
itself is informative.**

- Two non-human cases reach **PARTIAL-to-FULL** on exactly the clusters that matter most
  (Pepperberg's parrot on the logical/negation cluster; Herman's dolphins on the structural/
  syntax cluster) — but both are contested in the published literature, both involve N=1–2 trained
  captive individuals, and neither has been independently replicated at the scale the vervet/
  bee-dance/birdsong findings have. This is real evidence, not nothing — but it does not rise to
  the same evidentiary standard as the uncontested cases.
- The clean, uncontested non-human evidence (vervet calls, bee dance, birdsong, elephant names)
  stays exactly where Phase 0 already found it: reference and graded/hierarchical-bounded
  compositionality, yes; full negation/quantification/embedding, no.
- The one clean **non-cognitive** case (bacterial promoter logic) is genuinely important: it shows
  Boolean-like *structure* can exist without any mind at all — but explicitly does not show
  *meaning* in the semiotic sense, which sharpens rather than weakens the Peircean/biosemiotic
  boundary already proposed.
- The plant/mycorrhizal case is not just unconfirmed — it's actively been walked back by the field
  itself, which matters for calibrating how skeptically to treat similarly speculative claims
  elsewhere in this investigation.
- The strongest single finding in this phase is **human, not non-human**: Nicaraguan Sign Language
  shows full compositional generativity emerging reliably, across independent child cohorts, with
  zero transmitted model — meaning the "how rare is the threshold" question has a real, well-evidenced
  partial answer for humans specifically (not rare, given the right minimal social conditions), even
  though it says nothing about whether the threshold is crossable by non-human life generally.

## What this means going forward

The honest, sharpened conclusion for Phase 6's synthesis: full relational-algebra structure is not
confirmed anywhere outside contested, captive, small-N cases — but it is not cleanly absent either.
The strongest scoped claim the evidence actually supports is closer to: *reference and bounded
compositionality are broadly available in life; graded/parametric geometric encoding of relational
content is broadly available in life; the negation/quantification/embedding threshold has been
reached, contestedly, only in heavily-trained captive individuals of two species (an African Grey
parrot, two bottlenose dolphins) — and reliably and robustly in humans given only minimal social
transmission conditions (not requiring genius, culture, or inherited language).* That's a real,
citable, falsifiable position — not the strong "transcends all cognition and substrate" claim, and
not a clean negative either.
