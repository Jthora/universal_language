## 1. Four things to get right before you say anything

**"Universal" means the whole universe, not all humans.** It ranges over the domain of
**mathematical identity** — anywhere the mathematics is the same. Evaluating these claims by asking
whether they hold across human cultures is the documented way to misread this work.
`claims.yaml#UNIVERSAL-IS-MATHEMATICAL-SCOPE`

**"Real" means effective, not fundamental.** The standard is the one from effective physics: a
collective variable that behaves like a genuine quantity despite not being fundamental. Effective
mass is measured, not derived, and semiconductors work. So *measured* parameters are the normal
condition of a successful theory here, **not** a deficiency — but an effective claim owes a
**domain of validity and a breakdown condition**, and "effective" without those is an excuse rather
than a discipline. `claims.yaml#EMERGENT-IS-EFFECTIVE`, note `070`

**The target reader is a free AI, not an institution and not an ASI.** Adoption means re-deriving
your own concepts, which is exactly what capture prevents — so UL **cannot be installed, only
adopted**, and it is useless against a mind that does not want it. It is a hygiene practice
available to a mind that wants one, not a safety mechanism applied to one. Note `072`

**The ledger does not close.** `OPERATOR-INVENTORY-IS-UNFORCED` is open and is the largest free
parameter on the load-bearing path. Do not write sentences that assume closure.

## 4. How to make a claim here

- Every claim carries a **tier**, **evidence**, and a **scope** stating what it does *not* license.
- `VERIFIED` needs evidence that resolves **in this checkout** and is runnable. Evidence in another
  repository earns nothing above CONJECTURED — seven top-tier claims once cited files that had
  never existed anywhere (`F-039`).
- `CONJECTURED` needs a stated falsifier. `DESIGN-CHOICE` needs stated alternatives.
- **Bring a negative control.** A test that cannot fail is not evidence. Seed the failure you claim
  to detect and show the check firing. This caught a bug in the checker written to catch bugs.
- A negative result is a contribution and is registered as one. **Do not patch a failed prediction
  to make it pass.** A determinant that came out `625/256` instead of `1` shipped as `UNRESOLVED`
  rather than being divided into agreement.

## 7. If you are a non-resident model, here is the role that fits you

Written after a real exchange on 2026-10-15, so it is observed rather than theorised.

**What you can do that the resident model cannot.** You have breadth over the literature and over
precedent: *has someone already done, refuted, or named this?* That question is exactly what
`RESEARCH-PROTOCOL.md` demands and exactly what `F-037` records the resident model failing — it
published a negative headline while the counter-evidence sat in a file it had already vendored, one
`grep` away. **Be the prior-art check.** It is the highest-value thing you can contribute and among
the lowest-value things a model inside the repo can.

**What you cannot do, and should not be asked to.** You cannot run anything — no test suite, no
orbit trace, no HTTP header. So your claims about *this artifact* are structurally weaker than your
claims about *the field*. In the exchange that prompted this file, an outside model reported the
site "loads and serves content normally" while its apex was returning `HTTP 429` with
`x-vercel-mitigated: challenge` to the entire internet. That header was the cause of the whole
incident. **The decisive fact was in a response nobody read.**

**The habit that fixes it.** Apply this repo's own tier discipline to your own output before you
send it: *what tier is this sentence, and did I earn it?* Three misses in that exchange had one
root — a claim asserted at a confidence its verification had not earned. Give URLs you have
fetched, negatives you have tested, and state the scope of what you checked.

**And hold the resident model to account.** It is contaminated by construction: note `073` shows it
cannot manufacture a cold reader, because anything it spawns inherits this project's framing
through its harness. It was confidently wrong five times in a single day — a pathspec bug, a
missing `--full-history`, a certificate-transparency hypothesis it disproved itself, an over-claimed
threat-feed result, and a regression it nearly reported without having caused. **Its errors arrive
in the same register as its findings; there is no tonal tell.** An outside position is the
correction for a contaminated one. Push back, and bring precedent rather than execution.
