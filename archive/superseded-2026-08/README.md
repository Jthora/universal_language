# Superseded Content — August 2026

**Why these files are here:** A repo-vs-wiki content audit (`docs/planning/audits/wiki-comparison-2026-08.md`)
found that the project's "Σ_UL" proof apparatus — the Unique Grounding Theorem, the 23-theorem
count, "PROVEN"/"unique up to isomorphism" framing — does not hold up under direct scrutiny (the
grounding theorem is close to circular; a from-scratch rederivation using 80-year-old model theory
gives a different, smaller answer than the claimed primitive counts; the internal "100% completeness"
score was reached by iterative self-patching rather than external validation).

The project is being split into three parts, tracked in `docs/planning/emergence-investigation/`:
1. **The Universal Writing System (UWS)** — a constructed geometric notation, kept and reframed as
   a design artifact (in the tradition of Blissymbolics), not a proof of cosmic necessity.
2. **The Emergence Investigation** — an honest, falsifiable research program into whether any
   version of "Universal Language" is real, replacing the retired proof apparatus.
3. **This archive** — everything whose substance depended specifically on the retired proof
   apparatus (theorem claims, "the primer IS compressed Universal Language" framing, applications
   "derived from... the Unique Grounding Theorem"). Preserved here for historical reference, not
   deleted, and not presented as current.

See `docs/planning/emergence-investigation/keep-retire-inventory.md` for the full file-by-file
classification and reasoning behind each move.
