  format **by derivation rather than convention**, bootstrapped from the natural tier (UP).

  format; a language whose format is the universe's invariants installs the universe's coordinates.
  **A Universal Perspective/Perception enabling language** — scoped to format acquisition (the
  strong "purification" reading was killed by the far-transfer record *before* registration), with
  one registered prediction: by common-elements theory itself, a language of universal elements is
  the unique best case for broad transfer.

Quantum (linear resource discipline), Programming (meaning as behavior), Language (the full stack).

so drift is self-detectable — implicit convergence (which is already happening in artificial minds,
per the Platonic Representation Hypothesis) has no anchor, and drifts silently.
